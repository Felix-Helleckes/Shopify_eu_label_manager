# 27 September 2026: the EU legal-guarantee notice and the GARAN label become mandatory – what Shopify merchants must do now

*By Felix Helleckes · 11 September 2026 · Not legal advice*

Since 19 June 2026 every online shop selling to EU consumers must offer a withdrawal button. On 27 September the second
wave arrives: merchants must remind consumers of the legal guarantee of conformity with an officially designed notice
and must flag producer durability guarantees of more than two years with the new GARAN label. The European Commission
fixed every detail in Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1960: colours, font, QR code, wording. None of it may be edited.

## What applies from 27 September

**1. The harmonised notice on the legal guarantee.** A poster in EU blue, at least A4 in physical shops, in colour and
"in a prominent manner" online. It reminds consumers that goods sold in the EU carry a minimum two-year legal guarantee,
that the seller is liable, and that repair, replacement, price reduction or refund are available. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(l)
of the Consumer Rights Directive as amended by Directive (EU) 2024/825.

**2. The GARAN label.** If the producer offers, free of charge, a durability guarantee of more than two years covering
the entire good, the merchant must display the harmonised label: the title "GARAN", a tick, a calendar symbol, the
duration in years, brand and model identifier, a QR code to the Your Europe portal, and "producer guarantee in years"
in all 24 official languages. Online the label may be nested: a slim strip first, the full label on the first click
or roll-over.

**3. Since 31 July 2026: right to repair.** For new sales contracts consumers may choose between repair and replacement;
after a repair the legal guarantee is extended once by twelve months.

## What about the withdrawal button?

Already law. Art. 11a of the Consumer Rights Directive requires a function labelled "withdraw from contract here",
continuously available, with a second step labelled only "confirm withdrawal", followed immediately by an
acknowledgement of receipt with date and time.
